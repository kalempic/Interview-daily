## Question

Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate about a technical approach. What did you do, and how did it turn out?

## What interviewers check

- A specific situation, not a slogan about being a team player.
- Actions that show you listened, then did something concrete.
- A result, without invented numbers or private company details.

## Strong answer

Use STAR and keep it specific. Situation: name the kind of project and the constraint in one or two sentences, without confidential details. Task: say what you personally were responsible for.

Action: describe what you did. Read their proposal, restate their goal to be sure you heard it, share a concrete risk or an alternative, and agree on a decision rule such as a small spike or a metric you will both watch.

Result: say what shipped and what you would repeat, including a result that is not "I won the argument". Do not invent metrics, job titles, or employer names to make the story sound bigger.

## Rubric

- Sets a specific situation and your own responsibility
- Describes your actions, including how you heard the other person
- Ends with a concrete result and what you would repeat
- Stays specific and does not invent metrics or private details
